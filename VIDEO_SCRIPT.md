# Demo Video Script — "Finding San Diego's Future Climate Twin"
**Team Thunder4Champ: Zonglin Zhang · La Li · Vincent Gao — DSC 106**

Target length: **2:00 max**. Record a screen capture of the live GitHub Pages site with voiceover.
Aim for ~300 words of narration. Keep on-screen text minimal — let the visuals carry it.

---

### [0:00–0:08] — Title card (required opening frame)
**On screen:** Project title + all three names (the hero of the page works as the title frame).
**Narration:** *"Finding San Diego's Future Climate Twin — by Thunder4Champ."*

### [0:08–0:20] — HOOK
**On screen:** Hero section; mouse hovers the line "you'll hear one word: Phoenix."
**Narration:** *"Ask where San Diego's climate is heading, and you'll hear one answer: Phoenix. So we checked — with real climate models. They disagree."*

### [0:20–0:38] — MOTIVATION (why this is better than other explainers)
**On screen:** Scroll slowly into Step 1.
**Narration:** *"Most climate explainers give you a number — 'plus three degrees.' That's abstract. We answer it with a place you already know. Using real ERA5 observations for ten western cities and real CMIP6 projections for San Diego, we ask: which city today already feels like San Diego's future?"*

### [0:38–1:25] — THE VISUALIZATION (features + interactions + design)
**Step 1 — Trajectory (0:38–0:50):**
*"This is climate space. San Diego's path runs from today to the 2080s, drifting warmer toward the Southern California cluster — not toward the deserts on the right."* (Click 2050 → 2080s to show the path extend.)

**Step 2 — Ranking + sliders (0:50–1:05):**
*"Our Climate Twin Index ranks every city. The winner isn't Phoenix — it's Los Angeles, then Riverside. Phoenix scores near the bottom: its monsoon summers look nothing like San Diego's bone-dry ones."* (Drag a weight slider.) *"And you decide what 'similar' means — re-weight rainfall and watch it re-sort."*

**Step 3 — Map time-travel (1:05–1:15):**
*"Press play, and San Diego's climate physically travels — sliding north to whichever city it most resembles that year."* (Hit Play.)

**Step 4 — Fingerprint + uncertainty (1:15–1:25):**
*"Season by season against L.A. and Riverside — and the whiskers show the spread across climate models, so we're honest about what we don't know."*

### [1:25–1:52] — TAKEAWAY (the surprise / climax)
**On screen:** Step 5 — the best-match bar chart; point at the short SSP5-8.5 2080s bar.
**Narration:** *"Here's the part that surprised us. The best possible match holds near eighty for most futures — then, under high emissions by the 2080s, it collapses to fifty-six. San Diego doesn't just become Los Angeles. It eventually outruns every western city we have. The disappearance of a twin is the real warning."*

### [1:52–2:00] — Close
**On screen:** Scroll back to the takeaway headline.
**Narration:** *"San Diego isn't becoming Phoenix — it's becoming Los Angeles, and then leaving the map entirely."*

---

## Rubric coverage check
- **Hook** ✓ (Phoenix misconception, 0:08)
- **Motivation — why better than other explainers** ✓ (a familiar place vs. an abstract number, 0:20)
- **Explanation of features/interactions/design** ✓ (all 5 steps, sliders, play, uncertainty whiskers)
- **Most interesting/surprising feature** ✓ (Step 5 collapse — call it out as the surprise)
- **Takeaway + why the viz shows it well** ✓ (1:25)
- **Legible / ≤2 min** — record at 1080p, zoom the browser so labels are readable.

## Production tips
- Record the **published GitHub Pages URL**, not localhost.
- Do one slow practice scroll first; the scrollytelling triggers on scroll position.
- For Step 3, start the year slider near 2025 before pressing Play so the travel is visible.
- Export MP4 (H.264), upload **unlisted/public** to YouTube (not YouTube Kids).
